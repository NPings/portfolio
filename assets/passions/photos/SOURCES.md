# Photographer background photos

All ten photos are Nick Pings' own work, used as the background collage of the photographer section in `intro.html` (PRD 29.5, R61). No third-party images.

Processing: cropped to 4:5 around the subject, resized to 800x1000, saved as progressive JPEG (quality 74-82), all metadata stripped (no EXIF, no GPS). Originals were kept out of the repo.

Order is the order Nick sent them. With more frames than photos (15 at desktop), the collage repeats them in this order.
