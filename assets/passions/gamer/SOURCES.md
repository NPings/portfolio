## Sourcing notes (why each pick)

- **borderlands (replaced 2026-11-12, card t_83448adf):** the first pick (Xbox's GIPHY upload `gM6VrJEwKnw1To4WRe`) was 3.25 s of a Vault Hunter climbing into a buggy and pulling away, so it looped about twice per 7 s turn and read as a cutscene, not play (Nick's feedback). I re-checked every official source before falling back to Tenor. The Borderlands, 2K United Kingdom, Xbox, NVIDIA GeForce and PlayStation GIPHY channels hold about 60 Borderlands uploads between them; the gameplay ones are all 3.4 s or shorter except 2K UK's "burger launcher" clip (6.8 s), which is one stationary firing cycle shown twice. The rest are trailer cuts with a "BORDERLANDS 3" logo, character emotes, menus or title cards. The 2K Newsroom asset library (newsroom.2k.com/resources) lists Borderlands 3 and 4 trailers, but each one is only a YouTube link, and PRD 28.5 rules out downloading from YouTube. So this is a Tenor upload (tier 3): 11.3 s of continuous first-person Borderlands 3 play in Athenas, with the normal HUD and no face-cam, watermark or caption. The player steps out of an archway into the courtyard, aims down sights, fires, advances, reloads, and a grenade-style blast goes off down the street. Trimmed to 0.5-8.5 s (8 s) so a full 7 s turn plays without a repeat, ending on the blast. The rotation warms each clip up 1.5 s early, so later turns show about 1.5-8.5 s and only the last half second wraps. Source is 640 wide (Tenor's MP4 rendition), kept at 640. The fast motion needed CRF 29.5 to fit 900 KB, so it is soft at full screen; the 55% dim hides most of that. If 2K or Gearbox ever post a longer gameplay clip on GIPHY, swap it in. Takedown contact: the footage belongs to Gearbox Software / 2K; a request from them, from the uploader, or through Tenor's copyright process (Google's legal removal form) gets the removal recipe above, same day.
- **pokemon:** official channel, Legends: Z-A overworld (a trainer and Chikorita on a Lumiose street). Trimmed to the first 6 s, before the clip cuts to a staged camera shot.
- **fire-emblem:** no clean Fire Emblem gameplay exists on an official GIPHY channel (searches return fan art, anime-style edits and news graphics). This Tenor upload is Engage's own tactical map, attack forecast and battle, with no added text. Source is only 498 wide, so it is soft at full screen; the 55% dim hides most of that. Trimmed to 8 s.
- **smash-bros:** the Nintendo-tagged GIPHY Smash clips are under 1 s and tiny. This Tenor upload is a clean Ultimate match (Sephiroth vs. Captain Falcon), no gamertags or face-cam. Trimmed to 3.5 s so it ends before the finishing-hit zoom and "GAME!" banner (the brightest part).
- **destiny:** every official Bungie GIPHY clip is a trailer, emote, armor showcase or has promo text over it ("TWILIGHT ARSENAL / NEW VOID SUPER", plus a "PRE-ALPHA FOOTAGE" watermark). This Tenor upload is clean first-person combat. The source is 2.3 s, so the file is that loop played twice (4.6 s) to meet the 3 to 8 s rule.
- **baldurs-gate:** official Larian channel, party exploration along a stream with the normal HUD. GIPHY's 1080 rendition of this upload returns a "content not available" card, so the clip uses the 480-wide rendition (soft at full screen).

Clips at or under 640 wide (borderlands, fire-emblem, smash-bros, destiny, baldurs-gate) are worth replacing with sharper footage later, ideally Nick's own captures (PRD 28.8, logged in BACKLOG).
