## 2. Honest read: where v2 feels incohesive

1. **The page is a catalog, not an argument.** After the Practice section, the order is Impact → Work → Research case studies → AI case studies → Methods → Tools → Your session. Each section stands alone. Nothing builds toward "this person can run a research practice."
2. **Your second differentiator is split in two.** "AI in research, both ways" is the strongest thing you have, but its two halves sit in different lists. EVA (researching gen-AI for consumers) is under "Research" and the AI systems are under "AI Research Operations". A reader never sees them as one capability.
3. **Case studies are grouped by type, not by what they prove.** Research Self-Service is your clearest leadership arc (three systems, ending in the knowledge base). It's currently last in the second list. The three AI case studies are still mostly placeholders, so a leadership reader clicking them now gets "[Nick to add]".
4. **The hero undersells.** "Consumer research, and the AI behind it." is short and clean but positions you as a consumer researcher. The role is about running a practice, and Asana is B2B.
5. **The methods board reads as IC-level breadth.** 25 equally weighted cards say "I know many methods", which is a senior IC message. A Head of Research is judged on choosing methods for the question and combining them. The board's Operations column also repeats the Practice section. The scatter animation is fun, but it gives the most screen space to the least leader-like content.
6. **The tools marquee reads the same way.** 23 tool names are an IC skills list. A leader would mention tools as part of the stack they chose and run.
7. **The playful layer competes with the leadership message.** The session pill, the auto-opening observer panel, and the debrief show research thinking and personality, and they're distinctive. But the observer pops open at 3.5 seconds and its lines are jokey, which works against your plain voice rule and pulls attention from the hero. Worth keeping in a quieter form (see section 3).
8. **The Work section buries the leadership verbs.** The Experian entry has six dense bullets. "Rebuilt research operations" and "helped define Applied AI practices" sit among IC-phrased lines like "Partner directly with…".
9. **Small inconsistencies.** The resume subtitle says "Consumer Products & Research Operations" while the site says "AI Research Operations". Page titles mix "·" and "—". Original case studies end with "UX Research"; new ones end with "UX Research & AI Research Operations".

## 3. Proposed homepage structure

### Narrative spine

**I build research practices: I find what matters with the right mix of methods, then make it go further with operations and AI that hold to a standard.**

Every section either proves *find what matters* (craft and mixed methods), proves *make it go further* (ops and AI), or shows *how I lead* (standards, agenda, people). Anything that does none of these gets cut or folded in.

### Headline and positioning direction

- Eyebrow: describe the work, not a title. For example: "Research leadership · Mixed methods · Research operations · AI in research".
- Headline options (all must fit in 1–2 lines; I'll measure before choosing):
  1. "Research that reaches the decision, and the operations that make it scale."
  2. "A research practice, built to go further."
  3. "Mixed-methods research, run as a practice."
- Recommendation: option 1. It carries both differentiators and the leadership angle without claiming a title, and it drops "consumer", which narrows him for B2B roles. "Consumer" stays in the body copy as fact.

### Section order

Uses the existing facets and case-card components. |

### Cut, merge, add

- **Cut:** the tools marquee; the methods scatter board (replaced, see 7); the Impact numbers unless verified; the split into "Case Studies · Research" and "Case Studies · AI Research Operations".
- **Merge:** Practice into the two-differentiator panels; the methods board's Operations cluster into differentiator 01; the two case-study lists into Selected work.
- **Add:** a short "How I lead research" section; one line per case card on what it proves; a mixed-methods evidence slot once Nick supplies a quant or mixed example (R2).
- **Keep:** the palette, type, components, name treatment, expanding experience rows, and the debrief concept.
