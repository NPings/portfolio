Philosophy

S-01

Find what matters

Start from the decision on the table and choose the method that can answer it: qualitative, quantitative, or both.

Make it go further

Build the operations, standards, and AI that carry findings past the readout and into the room where decisions get made.

Practice

S-02
Apply AI strategically and safely

Models help with execution, like screening, tagging, and first-pass synthesis. Direction and the important decisions stay with a person in the loop.

Questions before methods

A study starts with the questions that the current open decisions depend on. Everything else branches from there.

Open access to findings

Researchers do the research. Stakeholders get flexible, unrestricted access to what we've found, and can ask about it in their own words, in the context of the problem they're trying to solve.

Cover the whole lifecycle

Strategy, discovery, launch, iteration, and the long maintenance tail after. Research belongs at every stage, and the methods adjust as needed.

Methods

S-05
Evaluation7
LLM-as-a-judge

A model makes the call when it safely can, inside fixed human checkpoints, and a person steps in when it can't.

Human-in-the-loop review

Researcher sign-off and manager approval before anything a model produced is released.

Model trust standards

Written rules for where a model is trusted and where a person decides.

Evaluating AI with users

Testing a generative AI assistant with real people across live builds, competitors, and prototypes.

Model-graded rubrics

Deterministic checks for what can be checked exactly, and a model-graded rubric for what can't.

Tool-call trace review

Grading what an agent actually called, not just whether its reply sounded right.

Regression suites

The same eval rerun before and after a fix, to prove the fix held.

Evaluative6
Usability testing

Watching people attempt real tasks to find where a design breaks down.

Rapid iterative testing

Testing, fixing, and retesting in short cycles so each change is validated as it's made.

Concept validation

Putting an early idea in front of users to check the premise before anything gets built.

Heuristic analysis

Expert review of an interface against established usability principles.

Comparative testing

Running identical tasks across competing designs to see which performs better.

Diary studies

Participants log experiences over days or weeks to capture behavior across time.

Quantitative6
Survey design

Structuring questions so the resulting data answers the question actually being asked.

Statistical analysis

Testing whether observed differences in the data are real or noise.

Experimental design

Structuring A/B and multivariate tests so results are attributable to the change.

Net Promoter Score

A single-question loyalty benchmark, tracked over time and read alongside qualitative signal.

Longitudinal studies

Measuring the same population repeatedly to see how attitudes and behavior shift.

Adoption studies

Measuring whether a released feature is found, used, and kept.

Operations6
AI-assisted synthesis

Models handle transcription, tagging, and first-pass analysis under defined review standards.

Research repositories

A searchable store of findings so prior work is reused rather than rediscovered.

Self-service enablement

Tooling and templates that let stakeholders answer routine questions without a full study.

Research standards

Documented expectations for method, sample, and rigor at each stage of the life cycle.

Mentorship

Onboarding and coaching researchers through study design review and craft feedback.

Stakeholder management

Aligning partners on the question, the method, and what the findings can and can't decide.
