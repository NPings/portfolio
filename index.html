Practice

S-01

I find what matters with the right mix of methods, then make it go further with operations and AI that hold to a standard.

Research operations

About two years rebuilding how research gets done at Experian, so findings reach the people making decisions and don't stop at the readout.

Access

Not every question needs a full study, so I built self-service access for stakeholders. It started as ChatGPT agents, moved to Condens and Slack, and grew into a research knowledge base. Research Self-Service

Standards & people

The rebuild also set research standards for method, sample, and rigor at each stage, and covered onboarding and mentoring for researchers joining the team.

AI in research, both ways

I research AI as a product, and I use AI to run research, with clear limits on each.

Researching AI

I led research on integrating generative AI into Experian's consumer experience. The recommendations shaped Experian's educational tools, including where its virtual assistant shows up. Experian Virtual Assistant

Using AI in research

I introduced AI-assisted screening, analysis, and synthesis, and set the standards for where a model is trusted and where a person decides. I helped define Experian's internal Applied AI practices, and work closely with the Applied AI team on pilots like the knowledge base and skill kits.

How I lead research

S-02
Start from the decision

Every study starts with the decision it has to inform. That sets the method, the sample, and how much rigor it needs. I cover the whole portfolio, flagship to edge case, at every stage of the life cycle.

Mixed methods

I choose qualitative, quantitative, or both based on the question. Qualitative work explains why. Quantitative work shows how much and how often.

[Nick to add]

A recent example of working with quant partners: the question, how you aligned on it, and how the qual and quant results came together.

Standards that hold

Research standards set the bar at each stage. With AI the standard is explicit: where a model is trusted, and where a person decides. In the knowledge base, researchers sign off and a manager approves before anything is released.

Grow the team's capability

I onboard and mentor researchers through study design review and craft standards. With skill kits, teammates choose where AI helps in their own work instead of waiting for me to build it.
